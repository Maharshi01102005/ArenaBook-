Why ArenaBook exists

Because booking a ground should not involve six phone calls and a screenshot of a WhatsApp reply.

One place for every ground in your city

ArenaBook is a web-based reservation system for sports venues. Players search turfs and courts by sport, city and budget, see live slot availability for any date, and pay online. Venue owners get a single admin panel where they manage listings, bookings, payments and customer reviews.

The platform was built as a Python and Django project for Infolabz IT Services. It uses a relational database with dedicated tables for users, profiles, locations, sport categories, venues, bookings, payments, reviews and contact enquiries.

How a booking is protected

No double bookings

Every reservation is checked against existing pending and confirmed bookings for overlapping hours before it is accepted.

Transparent pricing

The total is derived from the venue's hourly rate and the duration you pick, so there is nothing to negotiate at the gate.

Verified reviews

Only players with a confirmed booking at that venue can post a rating, and each player can post once.

Payment records

Each payment stores its method, status and transaction reference, and you can print an invoice from your bookings page.
